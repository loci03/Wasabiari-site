Tetris Beats privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

DRAFT: written assuming the game has no accounts, ads, analytics, or online features. Confirm before publishing.

Tetris Beats is made by Ivan (Wasabi Entertainment) in Boston, Massachusetts. This policy explains what the game does with your information. Short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device (and in your own iCloud backup if you use one). We cannot see them.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Third parties

We do not sell or share data with anyone. Apple may collect data under its own privacy policy when you download apps from the App Store.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online features are added, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.

Contact

Questions? Email support@wasabiari.com.
