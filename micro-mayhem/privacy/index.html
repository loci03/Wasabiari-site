Micro Mayhem! privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Micro Mayhem! is made by Ivan (WASABI Entertainment) in Boston, Massachusetts. This policy explains what the game does with your information. Short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device (and in your own iCloud backup if you use one). We cannot see them.

Microphone and speech recognition (optional)

Micro Mayhem! is played by touch. The microphone is used only if you turn on the optional Voice levels setting, which is off by default. Until you do, the game never uses the microphone and never asks for microphone or speech recognition permission.

If you turn Voice levels on, levels that support voice, such as Walk It (shout “HIDE!”), ask iOS for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Recognition runs entirely on your iPhone and only while that round is on screen. Your voice is never recorded, saved, or sent to us, to Apple, or to anyone else. If recognition doesn't catch the word, a loud shout also counts, which the game detects by measuring loudness only.

iOS shows its standard speech recognition prompt, which mentions that speech data may be sent to Apple. Micro Mayhem! asks only for on-device recognition, so your audio stays on your iPhone. You can turn Voice levels off at any time, and change these permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Children

Because we collect no information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Third parties

We do not sell or share data with anyone. Apple may collect data under its own privacy policy when you download apps from the App Store.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online features are added, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
