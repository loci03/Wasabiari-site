Beat Stack privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Beat Stack is made by Ivan (WASABI Entertainment) in Boston, Massachusetts. This policy explains what the game does with your information. Short version: there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Only the initials and scores you choose to post, and your moves during a battle, go to our server.

What we collect

Shared leaderboard. When you get a high score, the game asks for your initials. If you enter them, it sends those initials (up to 3 letters or numbers), your score, lines, level and game mode, plus your finish time in Sprint. We keep that with the date in a public top-50 list for each mode, which everyone who plays can see. Please use initials, not your name. If you’re offline, the score waits on your device and is sent when you’re back online.

Battles. In a head-to-head game, both players join a room with a 4-character code. Our server passes your initials and your moves to your opponent while the match runs. Battles are not saved.

Your IP address. Like any website, our server sees your device’s IP address when the game connects. We use it only to limit score uploads to 10 a minute, keep it in memory for about a minute, and never store it.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, volume, voice, ghost piece, AI difficulty and starting level), your replays and your own score list are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.

Third parties

We do not sell or share your data. The leaderboard and battle server runs on Cloudflare, which handles connection data under its own privacy policy. The game’s fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address under Google’s privacy policy. If the game comes to the App Store, Apple may collect data under its own policy when you download it.

Children

The game has no accounts and asks for nothing but optional initials, so we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
